How to Use the 9:16 Safe-Zone Overlay Checker

Verify vertical framing, lower-third captions, and call-to-action graphics before publishing—processed 100% locally in your browser with zero server uploads.

01. Load Image or Video Locally

Drag and drop any vertical JPG, PNG, WEBP, MP4, WEBM, or MOV file—or click Load Sample Frame. Files are decoded directly in memory using HTML5 FileReader and Canvas without leaving your device.

02. Compare Platforms & Scrub Frames

Switch between TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts overlays—or turn on Side-by-Side (3x) view. For videos, use the inline Play/Pause toggle and timeline scrubber to inspect exact subtitle timestamps.

03. Export PNG Guide for Editors

Adjust overlay opacity, toggle the Universal Safe Box, and click Export Check PNG. Drop the exported proof image onto a top reference track in CapCut, Premiere Pro, or DaVinci Resolve while aligning text.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about vertical video safe zones, aspect ratios, and local browser privacy.

A safe zone is the unobstructed central region of a 9:16 vertical video where captions, titles, faces, and call-to-action graphics remain completely visible without being covered by platform UI elements such as top navigation tabs, right-side engagement buttons, or bottom captions and audio tickers.

On a 1080 × 1920 px frame, TikTok reserves 11% (211 px) top, 20% (384 px) bottom, and 15% (162 px) on the right action bar. Instagram Reels reserves 10% (192 px) top, 18% (346 px) bottom, and 14% (151 px) right. YouTube Shorts reserves 9% (173 px) top, 19% (365 px) bottom, and 15% (162 px) right.
